Acumen, a global pioneer in impact investing, is changing the way the world tackles poverty since 2001.

With initiatives spanning eight global regions, Acumen combines philanthropic capital with localized strategies and business tools to prove that thriving social enterprises can make a change through innovative, market-based solutions.

As the organization transitioned into a new era of influence and innovation, Acumen has undergone transformational changes to its model and brand to solidify its position as a leader, creating a new platform to effectively communicate its mission and programs while inspiring global audiences to join its mission.
