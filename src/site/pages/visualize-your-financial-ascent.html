Charts

Visualizing their financial ascent

The campaign was created for Bank Sabadell's new Enterprise Account with a single, powerful message: "Charting a new trajectory for your company’s future."

This concept moves beyond traditional banking rhetoric, positioning the account not just as a financial product, but as a strategic tool for sustained financial and structural advancement. The purpose was to inspire large enterprises to adopt a new perspective—one that sees opportunities for growth, development, and profit where others see only business as usual, reinforcing the brand's position as a knowledgeable and empathetic partner that offers tailored solutions for its clients' success.

Visually, the campaign brings this new perspective to life by transforming ordinary office buildings into dynamic financial charts. Through clever cropping and framing, a building's architecture becomes a line chart representing growth, a circular facade becomes a pie chart for market share, and a series of towers transforms into a bar chart illustrating performance.

This striking visual language is supported by compelling headlines—such as “Unlocking your financial growth” and “Redefining your financial horizon”—that directly connect the campaign's visuals to tangible benefits; demonstrating that with the right partner, every enterprise can see its own potential charted for a more prosperous future.
