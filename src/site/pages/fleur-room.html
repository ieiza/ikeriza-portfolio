Fleur Room

Situated in the heart of NYC’s Flower District, The Fleur Room is a tribute to its botanical surroundings, embracing floral motifs in both design and spirit.

Conceptualized by TAO Group for the opening of Moxy Chelsea, the space seamlessly fuses nature and nightlife, offering guests a blooming treat for the senses.

Already a magnet for celebrities like Idris Elba, Mick Jagger, and Lizzo, this venue has hosted Met Gala and MTV VMA afterparties, cementing its place as a star-studded icon.

With its luxurious details, unparalleled views, and vibrant atmosphere, The Fleur Room stands tall as a luminous gem in the firmament of New York nightlife.
